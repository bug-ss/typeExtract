SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Services Agreement is signed on March 3, 2026 between Acme Tecnologia Ltda. (the Client) and
Beta Consultoria S.A. (the Provider), with Maria Souza as witness.

1. The Provider shall deliver the monthly compliance reports described in Annex I.
2. The Client shall pay the Provider a total of $180,000.00 in twelve monthly installments of
$15,000.00, each due by the 10th day of the month.
3. Late payments accrue a fine of 2% plus interest of 1% per month, as allowed by Law No. 10.406/2002.
4. Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days' written notice.
